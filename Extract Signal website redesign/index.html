Methodology

01

Extract at the source

We work upstream of lists and lead exchanges — monitoring primary-source data and isolating the events that indicate someone is preparing to act, before that intent is diluted into a shared list.

Every record is checked for identity, eligibility and timing against your written criteria. What doesn't clear the bar is discarded — never discounted and passed along anyway.

Qualified opportunities arrive in your CRM as structured records, each carrying its source and qualification trail. Delivered once, to one client — no shared lists, no resale.
